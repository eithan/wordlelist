When Wordle Started Repeating

For its first 1,689 puzzles, from June 19, 2021 to February 1, 2026, Wordle never used the same answer twice. That changed on February 2, 2026, when Wordle #1,689 brought back CIGAR: the very first Wordle answer, from June 19, 2021.

Since then, 27 of 256 puzzles (10.5%) have reused a past answer, about 1 in 9. No word has been the answer three times yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Wordle repeat answers?

Yes, since February 2, 2026. For its first 1,689 puzzles Wordle never reused an answer. Since then the New York Times has reused 27 past answers.

How often does Wordle repeat words?

About 1 in 9 puzzles since repeats began: 27 of the 256 puzzles from February 2, 2026 to October 15, 2026 reused a past answer.

Has today's Wordle been used before?

The banner at the top of this page tells you without revealing the word. It checks today's answer against every past Wordle answer and switches to the new puzzle at midnight in your timezone.

Has a Wordle word ever been used three times?

Not yet. Every repeat so far has been the second time that word was the answer.
